# Growing Calendar — Dallas (zone 8a/8b)

*A season-by-season anchor crop list for the [growing program](30-growing-program.md), tuned to Dallas and to a K–5, all-four-priorities, grow-through-summer program. A **starting draft for the science/garden teacher to refine** — not gospel.*

> **Frost dates (approx., verify locally):** last freeze **~mid-March**, first freeze **~mid-to-late November**. Two strong shoulder seasons (spring + fall) bracketing a brutal summer — which we grow through with heat-lovers.

---

## The year at a glance

| Window | Months | Mode | Anchor crops |
|--------|--------|------|--------------|
| **Late winter → spring** | Feb–May | Cool→warm transition | Cool crops finishing; warm crops planted after mid-March frost |
| **Summer** | Jun–Aug | **Grow-through (heat-lovers)** | Okra, southern peas, sweet potato, peppers, eggplant, Malabar spinach, basil |
| **Fall** | Sep–Nov | Cool season (the best window) | Greens, brassicas, roots, peas — Dallas's *prime* growing season |
| **Winter** | Dec–Jan | Low / protected | Hardy greens under cover; freeze protection on the few hard nights |

## By crop priority

**① Kid-engagement (fast, visual, tasty):**
- **Radishes** (~25 days — instant gratification), **lettuce**, **sunflowers** (tall, dramatic), **pole beans** (climb the trellis), **cherry tomatoes**, **snap peas**, **pumpkins/gourds** (fall, dramatic), **strawberries**.

**② Edible / take-home:**
- Spring/summer: tomatoes, peppers, beans, squash, cucumbers, basil.
- Fall/winter: lettuce, kale, chard, carrots, broccoli, cabbage, beets, cilantro.

**③ TX natives & pollinators (feeds the [nursery program](30-growing-program.md)):**
- **Bluebonnet** (state flower — needs fall sow + seed scarification = a great lesson), **black-eyed Susan**, **purple coneflower (Echinacea)**, **milkweed** (monarchs!), **Mexican hat**, **Turk's cap**, **Salvia greggii**, **lantana**, **winecup**.
- ⚠️ Many natives want **fall sowing / cold stratification** — *that timing is itself a curriculum moment*, and it shapes when the nursery batches start.

**④ Research / demo:**
- **Root-window crops:** potatoes (tubers!), carrots/radishes (taproot) vs. beans/grasses (fibrous) — the taproot-vs-fibrous comparison the teacher wanted.
- **Hydroponic-vs-soil:** lettuce or basil grown both ways, side by side.
- **Selection experiment:** the natives "plant 11, keep your best" grading lesson.

## Planting cadence (rough)

- **Aug–Oct:** sow the fall garden (greens, brassicas, roots) — Dallas's strongest season; also **fall-sow natives**.
- **Jan–Feb:** start warm-season seedlings indoors at the seedling station; sow late-winter cool crops.
- **Mid-Mar (after last frost):** transplant warm-season crops.
- **May–Jun:** transition to heat-lovers for the **grow-through summer**.
- **Year-round:** succession-sow fast crops (radish, lettuce) so every class rotation has something to do/harvest.
