# SOP-01 · Daily Check

**Who:** Teacher with students · **When:** Every school day, ideally morning · **Time:** ~5 minutes

> Goal: catch problems early and keep everyone safe. A quick, every-day habit beats a deep check no one does.

---

## ⚠️ Before anyone goes inside — heat & safety

1. **Look at the door/wall thermometer or your phone app first.** A closed greenhouse can be dangerously hot even on a mild day.
2. **If it feels hot or reads above ~90 °F: open the door and roll up the sides to vent it BEFORE students enter.** Let it breathe for a few minutes.
3. **Never let the door latch behind anyone.** It must open from inside, always.

## The 5-minute walk-through

- [ ] **Temperature OK?** Comfortable inside, vents/fans responding. 📷
- [ ] **Plants look happy?** No major wilting, yellowing, or pest damage. Wilting at mid-morning usually means *water*. 📷
- [ ] **Did the water run?** Soil/media damp, not bone-dry or swamped. Reservoir not empty.
- [ ] **Rainwater tank** has water in it (glance at the gauge/level). 📷
- [ ] **Battery is charging / charged** (check the indicator). 📷
- [ ] **Nothing broken, leaking, or in the walkway.** Floor not slippery.

## ✅ What "good" looks like
Comfortable temp, perky plants, damp soil, water in the tank, battery healthy, clear floor.

## 🚩 If something's wrong
- **Too hot / fans not running →** vent it manually (door + sides), then check the battery and the controller. If it won't cool, call the on-call volunteer.
- **Plants badly wilted & soil dry →** water by hand now, then check why irrigation didn't run (timer, tank empty, pump).
- **Tank empty / battery low →** note it for the weekly check; if both are bad on a hot day, escalate.
- **Anything you can't fix in 5 minutes →** log it and call the next person on the chain. Don't let it ride.

## 📓 Log it
One line in the logbook: **date · your name · "all normal" or what was off.**

---
*Escalation: on-call volunteer → backup → staff lead (numbers by the door). Ties to [Operating Principles 4 & 6](../00-operating-principles.md).*
