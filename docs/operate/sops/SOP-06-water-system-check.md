# SOP-06 · Water-System Check

**Who:** 🟢 Green / volunteer (routine) — escalate issues to 🟠 Amber · **When:** Weekly · **Time:** ~15 min

> Keeps the gravity water system flowing and clean. Most of this is "look + flush a valve" — no tools, no power. (System: [resource cycles](../../design/70-resource-cycles.md).)

---

## 1. Tank levels (top to bottom)
- [ ] **HIGH:** white + pure tanks have water. 📷
- [ ] **MEDIUM:** grey reuse buffer not stagnant (turning over, no smell).
- [ ] **LOW:** cistern level — note it **against the week's forecast** (top up via the campus tether if a dry/hot stretch is coming).

## 2. Flush the sediment filters
- [ ] **Open the flush valve** on each spin-down filter (coarse ~500 µm *before* the pump; then ~100 µm and ~50 µm *after*) until the water runs clear, then close. 📷
- [ ] Coarse-first means quick flushes — if one clogs fast, the settling upstream needs attention.

## 3. Settling + basins
- [ ] **Sink settling tank / sediment trap:** scoop the settled soil → **back to a bed/compost** (don't bin it).
- [ ] **Fogger basin:** float valve holding the level, water clean, fogger misting; **overflow path clear**.
- [ ] Confirm the **managed-spill cascade** isn't blocked: grey buffer → beds → swale.

## 4. Safety + integrity
- [ ] **All tanks/basins covered** (mosquito + child safety, [REQ-WATER-2](../../design/10-requirements.md#e-irrigation--water)).
- [ ] **Air-gap intact** on the campus-tether fill — the hose discharges *above* the white tank's water line, **not** submerged (backflow protection). 📷
- [ ] Spot-check drip lines/emitters — flowing, not clogged.

## ✅ What "good" looks like
Tanks at sensible levels, filters flushed clear, basins clean and covered, air-gap intact, water moving where it should.

## 🚩 If something's wrong
- **Won't flow / low pressure** → check tank level + a clogged filter first (flush it).
- **Grey buffer smells / stagnant** → it's not turning over; use it down (water beds) and tell the staff lead (may be oversized or under-used).
- **Pump not lifting to the white tank, controller/valve issues, persistent clogging** → 🟠 Amber ([SOP-04](SOP-04-something-isnt-working.md)).

## 📓 Log it
One line: **date · name · tank readings (cistern/white/grey) · filters flushed · anything off.**

---
*Most tasks are Green-doable (flushable spin-downs, visual checks). Pump/controller faults are Amber. Ties to [Operating Principle 4](../00-operating-principles.md) (trust but verify).*
