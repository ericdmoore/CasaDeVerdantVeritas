# SOP-02 · Weekly Check

**Who:** Volunteer · **When:** Once a week, year-round · **Time:** ~30 minutes

> Goal: *trust but verify the automation.* The daily check confirms things look fine; this check confirms the systems that keep plants alive are actually working — before a silent failure becomes a dead greenhouse.

---

## Safety first
- [ ] Vent the space before working in it if it's warm (door + sides). Work in the cooler part of the day in summer.
- [ ] Bring water; tell someone you're here if you're alone.

## 1. Verify the automation actually works
- [ ] **Irrigation:** confirm the last cycle ran (controller log, or damp soil at several beds). Walk the lines for clogs/leaks. 📷
- [ ] **Ventilation:** watch the powered vents/fans cycle. Trigger a test if you can.
- [ ] **Passive openers:** confirm the wax-piston auto-vents move freely (they're the no-power backup — they must work).
- [ ] **High-temp alarm:** test it. If it doesn't sound/alert, fix before you leave.

## 2. Power (off-grid)
- [ ] **Battery state-of-charge** healthy and trending up over the week, not sliding down. 📷
- [ ] **Solar panels** clean and unshaded (dust, leaves, bird mess cut output). Wipe if needed.
- [ ] Connections dry and secure; enclosure closed and out of reach.

## 3. Water (off-grid)
- [ ] **Rainwater tank level** — enough for the week ahead? Note it against the forecast.
- [ ] Gutters/downspouts to the tank clear.
- [ ] If using evaporative cooling: **clean the pads/filter**, confirm the pump runs and water distributes evenly.

## 4. Plants & space
- [ ] **Scout for pests/disease** — check leaf undersides; act early. 📷
- [ ] Remove dead leaves/debris; tidy walkways (slip + pest control).
- [ ] Top up reservoirs / nutrients per the current grow plan.

## 5. Stock & wrap-up
- [ ] Glance at the **spares shelf** (timer, fan, tubing, fittings) — reorder anything used.
- [ ] **Log it:** date · name · what you tested · anything off · anything reordered.

## ✅ What "good" looks like
Every automated system demonstrated working, battery trending up, tank adequate for the week, no pest outbreak, spares stocked.

## 🚩 If something's wrong
- **A system failed its test →** fix it now if it's a known spare/quick fix; otherwise escalate to the maintenance contact. A failed vent/fan/alarm in summer is urgent.
- **Battery sliding down week over week →** flag to staff lead; may be panels, a load left on, or a failing battery. Don't wait for it to die on a hot day.
- **Tank low with a hot/dry week forecast →** arrange a top-up (see [SOP-03](SOP-03-summer-survival.md)).
