# SOP-04 · Something Isn't Working

**Who:** 🟢 Green (any volunteer, teacher, student) · **When:** Whenever an expected behavior isn't happening · **Time:** ~2 minutes

> Your job is **not** to fix the electronics — it's to **notice and report well**, so an 🟠 Amber volunteer can fix it fast. Good signal beats a guessed fix. (See [roles & tiers](../10-roles-and-tiers.md).)

---

## First: is anyone in danger or is it getting hot?

If the greenhouse is **too hot** or the **high-temp alarm** is going: this is urgent — follow [SOP-01](SOP-01-daily-check.md#️-before-anyone-goes-inside--heat--safety) / [SOP-03 Emergency](SOP-03-summer-survival.md#d-emergency-response) (open the door + sides, vent it) **and** send the SOS below. Don't wait.

## Send a good SOS

Tell the on-call 🟠 Amber volunteer (numbers by the door), with:

- [ ] **What you expected** — "the fans should be running," "bed 3 should have watered."
- [ ] **What's actually happening** — "fans are off and it's hot," "soil in bed 3 is dry."
- [ ] **When you noticed** — and whether it was fine earlier.
- [ ] **Anything obvious** — a tripped look, a loose cable, a dark screen, a noise. 📷 **Snap a photo.**
- [ ] **Did anything change?** — storm last night, something got bumped, a long cloudy stretch.

> You **do not** need to know which sensor or wire. The system tracks which parts have "gone quiet," and the Amber runbook uses that to find the culprit. Your photo + plain description is exactly enough.

## The one hardware thing you *can* safely do

If something got **unplugged**, you may **re-seat keyed power** (the color-coded Anderson Powerpole / M12 connectors). They only fit the right way — if it doesn't seat easily, **stop** and report it; don't force it.

> ⚠️ **Do not** open enclosures, touch the battery/solar gear, or unplug anything you can't clearly identify. That's 🟠 Amber / 🔴 Red work.

## Log it
One line: **date · your name · what you reported · who you told.** So the next person sees it's known and being handled.
