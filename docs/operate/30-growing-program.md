# Growing Program

*What we grow, who grows it, where it goes, and how the program funds and grows itself. The operating embodiment of the four missions ([education, food, community/showcase, research](../../README.md)). Crop specifics live in the [growing calendar](40-growing-calendar.md).*

> **Lead:** the school's **science/garden teacher** (the program champion). **Participants:** all of K–5.

---

## Season-of-operation: grow through summer

We **grow through the summer** (not rest the beds) — which makes summer staffing load-bearing:

- **Summer crew = the garden teacher + nearby neighborhood volunteers.** Local proximity is the key — summer coverage works because helpers live close, not because of distant commitment.
- Summer planting leans **heat-tolerant** (okra, southern peas, sweet potato, peppers, eggplant) and **automation-tended** ([irrigation](../../design/10-requirements.md#e-irrigation--water) + passive cooling carry the load between visits).
- This raises the stakes on the resilience design — the [summer-survival SOP](sops/SOP-03-summer-survival.md) is the backstop.

## Engagement: two tiers

| Tier | Who | Involvement |
|------|-----|-------------|
| **Baseline (in-class)** | **Every K–5 class**, all year | Rotating in-class participation — planting, observation, harvest, tied to science lessons. Spreads the program across the whole school (resilient, not dependent on a few). |
| **Garden club** | Opt-in, higher commitment | Deeper, hands-on stewardship — the committed core. *(Evolves as the program matures.)* |

Maps onto the [volunteer tiers](10-roles-and-tiers.md): every student is a 🟢 Green participant; the garden club is the committed-Green core that grows into real stewardship.

## Harvest → take-home, not cafeteria

Harvest goes home with students (**"backpack Thursday"**-style) plus in-class **taste-tests** — *not* into cafeteria meals. This is deliberate: a district's food-service compliance can't easily absorb a small campus garden's produce (food-safety liability), and take-home delivers the "grow-your-own-food" win without that hurdle.

> Note: the **cafeteria-scraps-→-compost** loop ([resource cycles](../../design/70-resource-cycles.md)) is *separate and still on* — scraps come *in* for compost; produce just doesn't go *back* to the cafeteria.

## Crop priorities

All four, balanced (see the [calendar](40-growing-calendar.md) for the season-by-season list):
1. **Kid-engagement** — fast, visual, tasty (radishes, lettuce, sunflowers, pole beans, cherry tomatoes).
2. **Edible / take-home** — enough to send produce home.
3. **TX natives & pollinators** — ecology + the nursery program below.
4. **Research / demo** — hydroponic-vs-soil, the root window, selection experiments.

## The native-plant nursery program (long-term)

A self-funding flagship, run as a **PTA fundraiser — "Planting Seeds of Learning."**

- **The model:** each student sows a batch of TX-native seeds (e.g. "11 seeds"); **keeps their best one**, and the **next-best ~10 are grown on and wholesaled to a local nursery.** It's simultaneously a *selection/quality* lesson and a revenue stream.
- **Why PTA:** a PTA is a separate 501(c)(3); **plant-sale fundraisers are normal PTA activity**, which routes "selling product" out of district compliance and into the PTA's lane.
- **The flywheel:** sales → PTA funds → program expansion → more plants. **The budget grows with wholesale success.**
- **To line up:** principal/district sign-off to operate on campus, a **nursery wholesale agreement**, PTA financial controls; watch unrelated-business-income only if it ever scales large.

## Phasing (budget grows with success)

- **Phase 1 (core):** the greenhouse running — whole-school in-class growing, take-home harvest, compost + water loops, the built-in educational features.
- **Later phases (funded by the flywheel + structure/summer/automation experience):** the **native-plant nursery sales**, **aquaponics** (plumbing roughed-in now — see [zone layout](../../design/80-zone-layout.md)), more automation/connectivity, the garden club's expanded role.

## Built-in educational features

The teacher's wishlist — *most are construction-locked* (designed into beds/structure now; detailed in [zone layout](../../design/80-zone-layout.md) + [slab pre-pour](../../build/00-slab-prepour-checklist.md)):
**root window** (taproot vs. fibrous, potatoes) · **worm window** · small-scale **hydroponics** (visible reservoir) · **data station** · walk-under **tall-crop trellis** · **seedling station** (grow lights) · **repotting station** · **potting table with integrated soil-catch hole + catchment** (returns soil to beds) · **aquaponics rough-in** (future).
